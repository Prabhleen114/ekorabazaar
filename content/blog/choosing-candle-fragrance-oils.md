Choosing the right fragrance oil for candle making requires matching aromatic chemistry to your wax matrix, wick selection, and jar diameter—not simply buying a scent that smells good out of the bottle. Two fragrance oils at identical percentages can behave completely differently in [Natural Soy Wax 464](/products/854) versus [Paraffin Wax Slabs](/products/21).

## 1. Match Fragrance Behaviour to Your Wax Base

Different waxes hold fragrance oil at distinct saturation limits:

| Wax Base | Typical Safe Fragrance Load | Binding Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [464 Natural Soy Wax](/products/854) / [Soy Wax Chunk AA+](/products/862) | 6% – 10% | Lower melt point; needs thorough stirring at 70°C–75°C and 7–14 days of curing for strong hot throw |
| [Paraffin Wax Slab](/products/21) | 8% – 12% | Higher fragrance retention capacity and faster hot throw stabilization (2–4 days) |
| Soy-Coconut or Soy Butter Blends | 8% – 10% | Smooth glass adhesion and balanced cold-to-hot throw |

Calculate exact wax and oil weights in grams before melting using the [Candle Fragrance Load Calculator](/tools/fragrance-load-calculator). Never dose fragrance by volume or eye-balling, because woody and gourmand oils have higher density than light citrus blends.

## 2. Understanding Pour Temperature and Flash Point Timing

A fragrance oil's **flash point** is a combustion safety metric, not an evaporation threshold. However, lighter top notes inside low-flashpoint blends volatilize faster in hot studio air. When pouring into soy wax heated to 82°C–85°C to clear crystalline memory:

1. Cool the molten wax down to roughly 70°C–75°C.
2. Add your weighed [candle fragrance oil](/wholesale/candle-fragrance-oils) slowly while stirring steadily for two full minutes.
3. Cool further to your wax's target pouring range (often 58°C–65°C for soy container jars) before pouring.

## 3. Selecting Scent Profiles Across Indian Seasons

Warm Indian summers affect wax crystallization and surface sweating, while festival gift seasons drive heavier gourmand and traditional floral demand:

- **Fresh & Citrus Uplift:** [Midnight Blue Citrus Fragrance Oil](/products/588) and [Clean Ocean Fragrance Oil](/products/596) pair well with 7%–8% loads in soy containers.
- **Classic Indian Florals:** [Rose Candle Fragrance Oil](/products/591) and [Neem & Tulsi Fragrance Oil](/products/574) deliver botanical room presence without metallic undertones.
- **Gourmand & Cozy Bakery:** [Vanilla Straight Candle & Cosmetic Fragrance Oil](/products/624) and [Hazelnut Coffee Fragrance Oil](/products/509) offer strong room projection and benefit from step-up wick sizing because vanillin increases pool viscosity.

## 4. A Reliable 3-Step Small-Batch Test Protocol

Before buying bulk 500ml or 1 Litre studio cans, pour one 150g–200g test jar:

1. **Surface Inspection (48 Hours):** Check for amber oil beading ("sweating") on top of the wax. If oil pools, reduce fragrance load by 1.5%–2% or adjust addition temperature.
2. **Cure Window (7–10 Days):** Allow natural vegetable waxes time for crystal stabilization before lighting.
3. **4-Hour Melt Pool Test:** Burn in a closed room and evaluate both scent throw at 3 metres and flame height / mushrooming.
