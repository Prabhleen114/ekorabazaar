Launching a reliable scented candle line in India depends on getting four core raw material groups working in balance: **wax base**, **candle fragrance oil**, **calibrated wick**, and **heat-safe vessel or silicone mould**. Sourced carefully, every batch pours cleanly, burns evenly, and holds profitable margins.

## 1. Core Wax Bases ([Candle Waxes & Additives](/wholesale/candle-waxes-additives))

- **Container Soy Wax:** Lower melting point waxes like [464 Soy Wax Granules](/products/854), [Soy Wax White Chunk AA+](/products/862), and [Soy Wax White Flakes](/products/944) adhere well to glass tumblers and give smooth creamy tops when poured cool.
- **Paraffin & Pillar Waxes:** [Paraffin Wax Slabs](/products/21) shrink slightly on cooling for clean release from pillar and sculptural moulds while locking in high fragrance loads.
- **Gel & Specialty Waxes:** Transparent gel waxes support suspended botanical or embed aesthetics with mineral fragrance oils.

## 3. Wicks, Vessels & Moulds

- **Wicks ([Wick Systems & Hardware](/wholesale/wick-systems-hardware)):** Match braided cotton or wooden wick thickness to internal jar diameter—never pick one generic wick for 50g votives and 300g tumblers.
- **Glass Vessels & Tins ([Candle Jars & Containers](/wholesale/candle-jars-containers)):** Thermal-tolerant glass tumblers, amber jars, and travel tins ([Metal Containers & Tins](/wholesale/metal-containers-tins)).
- **Sculptural Pillar Casting:** Pair higher-melt waxes or beeswax blends with flexible [Candle & Pillar Moulds](/wholesale/candle-pillar-moulds).
