# Starting a Home Candle Business in India: Legal & Setup Guide

Over the past three years, the handcrafted home fragrance and scented candle industry in India has experienced exponential growth. What was once dismissed as a minor festive hobby has evolved into a multi-crore consumer lifestyle sector.

Indian consumers across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities are investing heavily in home ambiance, self-care rituals, mindful aesthetics, and premium gifting. The festive surge around Diwali, Durga Puja, Christmas, and the winter wedding season generates intense demand for bespoke, high-end scented candles.

Yet moving from pouring a few candles for friends and family to running a legally compliant, profitable home-based manufacturing enterprise requires clear operational clarity. You must navigate business registration, tax obligations, courier logistics across India's demanding transit routes, compliant packaging, and profitable unit economics.

This comprehensive guide delivers a practical, step-by-step roadmap to starting, branding, and scaling a home candle business in India from your kitchen or spare studio room.

## 1. Legal Foundations: MSME, GST, and Business Registration

Setting up your business legally from day one establishes credibility with suppliers, corporate clients, and payment gateways.

### 1. Business Structure: Sole Proprietorship
For 90% of solo makers starting out from home, a **Sole Proprietorship** is the simplest, most cost-effective legal structure:
- It requires no formal company incorporation fees or expensive annual audit compliances.
- You operate under your chosen brand trade name, linked to your personal PAN and Aadhaar.
- As your annual revenue scales past ₹25 to ₹50 Lakhs, you can easily transition into a Private Limited Company or One Person Company (OPC).

### 2. MSME Udyam Registration (Completely Free)
The Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises provides the **Udyam Registration** online:
- *Cost:* 100% free on the official government portal (`udyamregistration.gov.in`). Never pay private agents for this registration.
- *Benefits:* Unlocks access to collateral-free MSME bank loans, priority sector lending, government subsidy schemes, and legal protection against delayed commercial payments from B2B corporate buyers under the MSMED Act.

### 3. Understanding GST Rules in India
Navigating the Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a critical milestone:
- **Exemption Threshold for Goods:** Under current Indian GST law, if you sell goods exclusively within your home state (intra-state sales) through offline markets, pop-ups, or direct WhatsApp sales, you are **exempt from mandatory GST registration until your annual turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs** (₹20 Lakhs in northeastern and special hill states).
- **The E-Commerce Caveat:** If you sell inter-state across India through your own independent website (Shopify, WooCommerce) or marketplace platforms, obtaining a voluntary GSTIN is highly advantageous. It allows you to open a dedicated current bank account and claim full **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** on all the raw waxes, fragrance oils, and glass vessels you purchase through B2B platforms like [Ekora Bazaar](/shop).
- *Applicable Tax Slabs:* Decorative scented candles are categorized under HSN 3406 with an **18% GST** tax rate.

## 3. Mandatory Product Labeling and Safety Standards in India

Under the **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011** and the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**, any packaged good sold commercially in India must carry clear statutory disclosures:

### Essential Label Information:
1. **Generic Name of Commodity:** e.g., "Handcrafted Scented Soy Wax Candle".
2. **Net Quantity:** Stated by net weight in grams (e.g., "Net Wt. 200g / 7.05 oz") or count (e.g., "Contains 1 Unit").
3. **Month and Year of Manufacture:** e.g., "Mfg Date: October 2026".
4. **Maximum Retail Price (MRP):** Must explicitly state "MRP ₹XXX.00 (Inclusive of all taxes)".
5. **Manufacturer / Packer Details:** Name, complete registered address, and customer care contact details (email and telephone number).
6. **Safety Burning Warning Symbols:** Standard iconography depicting:
   - "Never leave burning candle unattended,"
   - "Keep away from children and pets,"
   - "Burn on a heat-resistant surface,"
   - "Trim wick to 6 mm before lighting."

## 4. Packaging for Indian Domestic Couriers (Zero Breakage Guarantee)

In India, domestic surface and air shipments travel over bumpy highways and undergo rigorous sorting hub handling. Glass breakage during shipping will destroy your brand reputation and drain margins through replacements.

### The 4-Layer Packaging Rule:
1. **Primary Vessel Protection:** Wrap the glass candle container tightly in 2 layers of 10mm bubble wrap or thick corrugated honeycomb paper.
2. **Individual Rigid Box:** Place the wrapped candle into a snug-fitting 3-ply corrugated mono-carton box with an interior cardboard collar that locks the lid in place.
3. **Master Shipping Shipper:** For multiple candle orders, pack individual cartons into an outer 5-ply shipping box filled with biodegradable starch packing peanuts or crumpled kraft void-fill. The boxes must not rattle or move when shaken vigorously.
4. **Fragile Warning Tape:** Seal with heavy-duty reinforced packing tape and affix large red "FRAGILE – HANDLE WITH CARE – GLASS" stickers on all sides.

### Logistics Partners:
Integrate with multi-courier shipping aggregators like **Shiprocket**, **Delhivery Direct**, or **NimbusPost**:
- Sourcing surface logistics costs roughly **₹60 to ₹110 per 500g package** across major Indian metro lanes.
- Provide automated WhatsApp tracking updates to customers to drastically reduce Cash-on-Delivery (COD) customer rejections and Return-to-Origin (RTO) rates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I run a candle business from a rented apartment in India?
Yes. As long as you respect basic residential peace and safety norms, running a clean artisan studio from a residential flat is standard practice. You are not operating heavy industrial machinery; you are using kitchen-scale double boilers and heat guns. Always maintain a certified dry-chemical fire extinguisher on site.

### Do I need a drug license or cosmetic license to sell candles?
No. Candles are categorized as home fragrance / decorative household goods, not topical cosmetics. They do not fall under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act and do not require CDSCO or state cosmetic drug manufacturing licenses (unlike soaps, lotions, or facial creams).

### How do I handle Cash on Delivery (COD) orders safely in India?
COD orders suffer from an average 15% to 25% Return-to-Origin (RTO) failure rate among Indian e-commerce brands. To protect margins:
1. Charge a flat ₹50 to ₹80 non-refundable convenience fee for COD orders at checkout.
2. Use automated WhatsApp confirmation bots to verify the customer's intent and full address before dispatching the parcel.
3. Offer a 5% instant discount for prepaid UPI payments to encourage upfront payment.
