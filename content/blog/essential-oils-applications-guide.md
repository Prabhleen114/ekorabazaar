Botanical [Essential Oils](/wholesale/essential-oils) are concentrated volatile extracts obtained directly from flowers, leaves, roots, bark, resins, or citrus peels by steam distillation or mechanical cold pressing. Because their chemistry differs dramatically from lab-blended fragrance oils, getting strong results in India requires matching each botanical oil to its ideal application.

## 1. Four High-Demand Studio Applications

| Application | Recommended Botanical Oils | Practical Usage & Caution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aromatherapy Diffusers** | Mandarin, Rosemary, Lavender, Eucalyptus, Tea Tree | 3–6 drops in ultrasonic water diffusers; store bottles shaded away from bathroom humidity |
| **Bath & Body / Massage** | [Sandalwood Essential Oil](/products/404), [Turmeric Root Essential Oil](/products/247), [Rosewood Essential Oil](/products/419) | Dilute conservatively (0.5%–2%) in carrier oil; avoid applying neat spice or citrus oils |
| **Artisan Cold-Process Soap** | Cinnamon Leaf, Clove Leaf, Lavender, Peppermint, Vetiver | Spice oils ([Cinnamon Leaf](/products/406), [Clove Leaf](/products/408)) speed up soap trace and carry tight dermal caps |
| **Natural Soy Candles** | Cedarwood, Patchouli, [Myrrh Essential Oil](/products/413) paired with citrus lift | Pure citrus oils ([Mandarin](/products/428)) evaporate quickly unless anchored with heavy resin or wood notes |

For gentler water-based skincare toners, facial mists, and cream phases, pair essential oils with aromatic [Ylang Ylang Hydrosol](/products/907), [Rosemary Hydrosol](/products/6820), or [Lavender Hydrosol](/products/5640).

## 2. Flash Point Volatility & Oxidation Care

Citrus peels (lemon, sweet orange, mandarin) are composed largely of light monoterpenes that oxidize within months if exposed to warm air and sunlight. Always:
- Keep amber or HDPE caps screwed tight immediately after weighing batches.
- Store reserve stocks below 22°C away from summer window heat.
- Record batch opening dates on studio inventory labels.

## 3. Sourcing & Compliance Basics for Makers

When producing skin-contact or aromatherapy retail products in India, avoid blending culinary or unproven oil sources into commercial lotions or balms. Verify product documentation when available, follow conservative IFRA dermal caps for sensitizing oils like cinnamon and clove, and test small 100g runs first.
