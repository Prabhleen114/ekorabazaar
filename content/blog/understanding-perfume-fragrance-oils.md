Independent Indian fragrance studios generally start with two core product formats: sprayable hydroalcoholic perfumes (Eau de Parfum / Eau de Toilette) and alcohol-free concentrated roll-on perfume oils or attars. Understanding how concentrated **perfume fragrance oils** interact with carrier solvents determines projection, clarity, and lasting power on warm skin.

## 1. Two Primary Perfumery Carriers Explained

### Perfumer's Denatured Alcohol (For Spray Perfumes)
Fine spray fragrances require clean, low-odor ethanol capable of dissolving aromatic compounds without leaving oily stains on clothing or smelling harsh at first spray:
- Use perfumery-grade 99% denatured ethanol bases like [Perfume Base Liquid (99% Ethanol Perfumer's Alcohol)](/products/790) or [Perfume Solvents Denatured Ethanol ABP001](/products/6329).
- Typical EDP concentration sits between **15% and 20% fragrance oil** by weight mixed into 80%–85% perfumer's alcohol.

### Dipropylene Glycol (DPG) & Oil Carriers (For Roll-On Perfumes & Attars)
Alcohol-free roll-on fragrances remain deeply popular across India because carrier mediums evaporate slowly and hold warmth close to pulse points:
- Clear cosmetic solvent [Dipropylene Glycol (DPG)](/products/617) ([bulk studio option](/products/2721)) or odorless fractionated coconut oil dilutes heavy fragrance oils to smoother viscosity for rollerballs.
- Avoid kitchen cooking oils that turn rancid or overpower top notes.

## 2. Building Scent Longevity With Pyramid Balance

When blending [Skin-Safe Fragrance Oils](/wholesale/skin-safe-fragrances) and [Pure Essential Oils](/wholesale/essential-oils), volatile top notes (citrus, mint, clean sea breeze) flash off within 15–30 minutes unless anchored by heavier middle and base notes:

- **Top Lift:** Citral/limonene notes such as [Mandarin Essential Oil](/products/428) or citrus-aquatic accords like [Blue Ocean Fragrance Oil](/products/499).
- **Heart Body:** Rich floral and botanical oils including [Rosewood Essential Oil](/products/419) or [Lavender Vanilla Fragrance Oil](/products/493).
- **Base Anchor:** Dense resins and woody accords like [Sandalwood Essential Oil](/products/404), [Myrrh Essential Oil](/products/413), or [Vetiver Fragrance Oil](/products/518) that slow evaporation for hours.

## 3. Maceration & Bottling Checklist

Freshly mixed alcohol perfumes often smell sharp until aromatic esters settle inside the ethanol lattice. Store blended concentrate + alcohol in sealed amber glass in a cool cupboard (18°C–24°C) for 2 to 6 weeks before filling retail bottles such as [10ml Roll-On Glass Perfume Bottles](/products/2656) or [10ml Glass Fine-Mist Spray Bottles](/products/2583). Always verify IFRA Category 4 (fine fragrance) dosage limits before launching skin-spray lines.
