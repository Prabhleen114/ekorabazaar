Whether you run a beginner gift-soap studio using pre-made glycerin blocks or formulate cold-process (CP) bars from raw oils and lye, sourcing consistent **soap-making raw materials** ensures smooth texture, stable scent, and clean demoulding.

## 1. Pre-Saponified Melt & Pour Bases vs Cold Process Fats

### Melt & Pour ([Artisan Soap Bases](/wholesale/artisan-soap-bases))
Melt-and-pour bases skip caustic lye handling and cure immediately after cooling. Core Ekora Bazaar options include:
- [Shea Butter Soap Base](/products/2413) — creamy opaque texture suited to mild moisturizing bar lines
- [Rose Soap Base](/products/2414) & [Multani Mitti Soap Base](/products/2417) — specialty pre-blended bases for floral or mineral bar collections
- [Lemon Soap Base](/products/2418) & Ultra-Clear Glycerin Bases — bright citrus and translucent embed formats

### Cold Process Fats & Additives ([Raw Butters & Carrier Oils](/wholesale/raw-butters-carrier-oils))
For from-scratch CP bars, combine hard saturated plant oils/butters (coconut, palm, shea, cocoa butter) with conditioning liquid oils (olive, sweet almond, castor) and sodium hydroxide calculated on a lye calculator.

## 3. Colorants, Botanicals & Moulds

- **Pigments ([Micas & Pigments](/wholesale/micas-pigments)):** Non-bleeding mineral micas and oxides stay crisp in multi-layer soap designs, whereas some synthetic water-soluble dyes migrate over time.
- **Dried Botanicals ([Dried Botanicals & Additives](/wholesale/dried-botanicals-additives)):** Reserve dried rose petals or calendula mainly for gentle surface garnishes (natural botanicals brown inside wet alkaline CP batter).
- **Moulds ([Soap & Bar Moulds](/wholesale/soap-bar-moulds)):** Silicone loaf moulds for hand-cut bars and multi-cavity silicone bar moulds for uniform retail weight.
