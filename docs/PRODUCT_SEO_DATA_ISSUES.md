# Product SEO & Data Quality Issues

## Overview
During the SEO audit, we scanned the database for product-content mismatches, copied descriptions, placeholder text, and keyword stuffing. 

## Identified Issues

### 1. Mango Butter / Cocoa Butter Mismatch
- **Issue**: The description for "Mango Butter" contains text clearly copied from or referencing "Cocoa Butter", leading to factual inaccuracies and confusing search engines.
- **Affected Products**: Unrefined Mango Butter, Refined Mango Butter (and potentially Shea Butter variations).
- **Recommendation**: Write a unique description for Mango Butter highlighting its specific properties (e.g., extracted from mango kernels, high oxidative stability, light texture).

### 2. Generic / Placeholder Descriptions
- **Issue**: Some products lack unique descriptions and use generic template text or manufacturer copy.
- **Affected Categories**: Artisan Soap Bases, Candle Waxes.
- **Recommendation**: Ensure every product has at least 50-100 words of unique descriptive text detailing its origin, usage, and benefits.

### 3. Missing Specifications
- **Issue**: Products in bulk categories (e.g., Fragrance Oils, Carrier Oils) are missing critical B2B specifications like flash point, specific gravity, or extraction method.
- **Recommendation**: Add a structured specifications section to the product description, which will be picked up by search engines and B2B buyers.

### 4. Shipping Language Conflicts
- **Issue**: Some legacy product descriptions or policy pages mention variable shipping costs or thresholds that conflict with the temporary flat ₹90 shipping rule.
- **Action Taken**: The global SEO schema and shipping policies have been updated to hardcode the ₹90 flat rate for accuracy.
