# OUTREACH.md — Ekora Bazaar Ethical Creator & Partner Outreach Playbook

## Compliance Notice & Editorial Standards
Ekora Bazaar strictly adheres to Google Search Essentials regarding link schemes and the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) guidelines on influencer disclosures:
- **No Link Exchanges for Free Product:** We never condition the provision of free product, samples, or discounts on an agreement to provide a backlink.
- **Editorial Freedom:** Creators retain 100% editorial freedom over whether they publish content, what opinions they share, and whether they cite suppliers.
- **Mandatory Link Attribution:** If a creator or partner chooses to link to Ekora Bazaar or a seller storefront as part of a seeded or sponsored collaboration, the link **must** use `rel="sponsored"` (or `rel="nofollow"`) in accordance with search engine quality guidelines.
- **Clear Disclosure:** Any post or video featuring gifted materials must visibly state `#gifted`, `#sponsored`, or "Materials provided by Ekora Bazaar" to adhere to ASCI Indian digital media norms.
